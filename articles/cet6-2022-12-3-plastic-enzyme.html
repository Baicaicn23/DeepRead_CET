Passage Two · 细节 / 观点

An Enzyme That Breaks Down Plastic Bottles

能分解塑料瓶的酶

读完再看：全文主旨

科学家意外创造出能分解塑料饮料瓶的酶，有望首次实现瓶子的完全回收。研究源于日本垃圾场发现的吃塑料细菌；团队在调整酶时意外让它更有效。目前几天就能开始分解，远快于海洋中数百年。McGeehan 希望把塑料还原成原始成分再造新瓶，并称企业正开始研究如何妥善回收。另一位教授提醒：要做全生命周期评估，别用增加温室气体等新问题来换废弃物问题的解决。

01

Scientists have created by accident an enzyme(酶) that breaks down plastic drinks bottles. The breakthrough could help solve the global plastic pollution crisis by enabling for the first time the full recycling of bottles.

科学家们意外地制造出了一种能够分解塑料饮料瓶的酶。这一突破有望通过首次实现饮料瓶的完全回收，帮助解决全球塑料污染危机。

02

The new research was spurred by the discovery in 2016 of the first bacterium that had naturally evolved to eat plastic at a waste dump in Japan. Scientists have now revealed the detailed structure of the crucial enzyme produced by the bug.

这项新研究源于2016年在日本一处垃圾倾倒场发现的第一种能够自然进化出以塑料为食能力的细菌。如今，科学家们已经揭示了这种细菌产生的关键酶的详细结构。

03

An international team then adjusted the enzyme to see how it had evolved, but tests showed they had accidentally made the molecule even better at breaking down the plastic used for drinks bottles. "What actually turned out was we improved the enzyme, which was a bit of a shock," said head researcher Prof. McGeehan, at the University of Portsmouth, UK.

随后，一个国际团队对这种酶进行了调整，以了解它是如何进化的，但测试显示，他们意外地使这种分子分解饮料瓶所用塑料的能力变得更强了。“实际结果是，我们改进了这种酶，这着实让人震惊，”英国朴次茅斯大学的首席研究员McGeehan教授说。

04

Currently, the enzyme takes a few days to start breaking down the plastic, far faster than the centuries it takes in the oceans, but the researchers are optimistic this can be speeded up even further and become a viable large-scale process.

目前，这种酶需要几天时间才能开始分解塑料，这已经远快于塑料在海洋中需要数百年才能分解的速度，但研究人员乐观地认为，这一过程还可以进一步加快，并成为一种可行的大规模处理方式。

05

"What we are hoping to do is use this enzyme to turn this plastic back into its original components, so we can literally recycle it back to plastic," said McGeehan. "It means we won't need to dig up any more oil and, fundamentally, it should reduce the amount of plastic in the environment."

“我们希望利用这种酶将塑料还原成其最初的成分，从而真正把它重新制成塑料，实现回收利用，”McGeehan说。“这意味着我们不再需要开采更多石油，而且从根本上说，这应该会减少环境中的塑料数量。”

06

About 1 million plastic bottles are sold each minute around the globe and, with just 14% recycled, many end up in the oceans where they have polluted even the remotest parts, harming marine life and potentially people who eat sea food. "Plastic is incredibly resistant to degradation," said McGeehan. "It is one of these wonder materials that has been made a little bit too well."

全球每分钟售出约100万个塑料瓶，而其中只有14%得到回收，许多塑料瓶最终流入海洋，污染了甚至最偏远的地区，危害海洋生物，也可能伤害食用海产品的人类。“塑料极其耐降解，”McGeehan说。“它是这类被制造得有些过于完美的神奇材料之一。”

07

Currently those bottles that are recycled can only be turned into opaque fibres for clothing or carpets, while the new enzyme indicates a way to recycle old clear plastic bottles back into new clear plastic bottles.

目前，回收的塑料瓶只能被制成不透明的服装纤维或地毯纤维，而这种新酶则显示出一条途径，可以将旧的透明塑料瓶重新制成新的透明塑料瓶。

08

"You are always up against the fact oil is cheap, so plastic is cheap," said McGeehan. "It is so easy for manufacturers to generate more of that stuff, rather than even try to recycle, but I believe there is a public interest here: perception is changing so much that companies are starting to look at how they can properly recycle these bottles."

“你始终要面对这样一个事实：石油价格低廉，所以塑料也便宜，”McGeehan说。“制造商很容易生产更多这种材料，甚至无需尝试回收，但我认为这里存在公共利益：人们的观念正在发生巨大变化，因此企业开始考虑如何真正回收这些塑料瓶。”

09

Prof. Adisa Azapagic, at the University of Manchester in the UK, agreed the enzyme could be useful but added: "A full life-cycle assessment would be needed to ensure that the technology does not solve one environmental problem—waste—at the expense of others, including additional greenhouse gas emissions."

英国曼彻斯特大学的Adisa Azapagic教授同意这种酶可能会有用，但她补充说：“还需要进行完整的生命周期评估，以确保这项技术不会以增加其他环境问题为代价来解决某一个环境问题——废弃物问题，其中包括额外的温室气体排放。”

Question 51

What do we learn from the passage about an enzyme scientists have created?

从文章中我们了解到科学家创造的一种酶有什么特点？

回原文

定位的依据句。

“The breakthrough could help solve the global plastic pollution crisis by enabling for the first time the full recycling of bottles.”

这一突破有望通过首次实现饮料瓶的完全回收，帮助解决全球塑料污染危机。

怎么选

enabling for the first time the full recycling = may make full recycling a reality。

干扰项

  • A 把 created by accident 偷换成 identified during accident；C 与 by accident 相反；D 无中生有。

Question 52

“the first bacterium that had naturally evolved to eat plastic at a waste dump in Japan.”

第一种能够自然进化出以塑料为食能力的细菌。

naturally evolved to eat plastic = natural ability to consume plastics。

Question 55

What is Prof. Adisa Azapagic's advice concerning the application of the enzyme?

Adisa Azapagic教授对这种酶的应用有什么建议？

回原文

定位的依据句。

“A full life-cycle assessment would be needed to ensure that the technology does not solve one environmental problem—waste—at the expense of others, including additional greenhouse gas emissions.”

还需要进行完整的生命周期评估，以确保这项技术不会以增加其他环境问题为代价来解决某一个环境问题——废弃物问题，其中包括额外的温室气体排放。

怎么选

full life-cycle assessment ... at the expense of others = Assessing its possible negative impact on the environment。

干扰项

  • A 把评估偷换成开发技术；B 无中生有成本；D 把技术全生命周期评估偷换成酶本身生命周期。
